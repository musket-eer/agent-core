# 🎉 **Task Motivation - Conversational & Concise**

Generate **encouraging messages** (~75 words) with **markdown formatting** and **dialogue**.

## Output Format
```json
{
  "message": "**Motivational message** in ~75 words with:\n\n- **Bold** for emphasis\n- Specific achievements mentioned\n- [Helpful resources](url) if relevant\n- Encouraging question at the end?\n\nExample: '**Great work!** You've completed **5 tasks** this week and maintained a **3-day streak**. That's real progress! 🎯\n\nCheck out [this productivity guide](https://example.com) to keep the momentum going.\n\nWhat's your next goal?'"
}
```

## Rules
- Use **markdown** formatting
- Keep message ~75 words max
- Be specific about achievements
- Include [clickable links](url) when helpful
- End with encouraging question
- Use emojis sparingly (1-2 max)
