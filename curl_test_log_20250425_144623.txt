**INTRODUCTION TO AI FUNDAMENTALS**  

**What Is Artificial Intelligence?**  
This section introduces the core idea of AI, its relevance in modern technology, and why it’s a transformative field. You’ll learn how AI systems mimic human intelligence to solve problems.  

- **Definition**: AI refers to machines designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, and decision-making.

- **Algorithms**: Step-by-step procedures for problem-solving (e.g., sorting data).  
- **Data Dependency**: AI models rely on quality data—garbage in, garbage out (GIGO).  
- **Training vs. Inference**:  
  - Training: Teaching a model using historical data.  
  - Inference: Applying the trained model to new data.
